## What the science has to produce

A sequence is a hypothesis. The project that is worth paying for turns it into evidence another scientist can attack.

Target-gene discovery from a genome or a transcriptome should say why this gene, why this pest, and why the neighbours of that pest are less likely to share the effect. dsRNA or siRNA design should be written down, including the sequences you rejected. A microbial expression system, E. coli being the common example, is a production hypothesis: can you make the RNA in a form you can formulate, at a scale that is not a single flask?

The bioassay protocol is where most startups are thin. One unreplicated pot is a demonstration. A protocol names the insect stage, the dose, the negative controls, the replication and the measurement. Off-target and non-target risk assessment is a design at this stage, not a claim that there is no risk. We design that assessment. The studies run at a laboratory we agree. We do not pretend one bench covers every non-target organism.

## What we will not claim

There is a result in this field that we will not restate until the metric and the trial scale are confirmed. Until then this site will not carry a percentage kill, a percentage reduction, or a comparison with a chemical standard. If you need that sentence for an investor, the work is to generate a number you have the right to show, not to borrow one from a conversation.

We also will not list foreign approvals as if we had re-verified each certificate on the morning of publication. Regulatory status outside India moves. The sentence you can rely on is the Indian one: there is no dedicated framework for sprayable dsRNA here yet. Anyone who tells a buyer that an RNAi spray is "already registered in India" should be asked for the statute and the certificate number.

## What the regulatory gap means in practice

It means you cannot buy a guideline checklist and fill it in. It means a CIB&RC microbial-pesticide guideline is the nearest existing language for a biological pesticide, and it may not fit a naked dsRNA spray. Forcing a new mode of action into the wrong form creates a file that answers questions nobody asked and skips the ones a future reviewer will.

The practical posture is:

1. Build identity, expression, bioassay and non-target design as if a reviewer will exist.
2. Write a pathway note that says "no dedicated framework yet", with the date you checked.
3. Watch the draft Pesticides Management Bill, 2025 as a proposal. The January 2026 draft talks about digital registration and accredited testing laboratories. It is not a dsRNA law, and it is not in force.
4. Do not sell a spray on a provisional biostimulant certificate. That certificate has been invalid since 17 Jun 2025, and it was the wrong regime anyway.
